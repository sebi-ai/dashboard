Dashboard — Your Personal Info Hub

This app turns any screen into a live, at-a-glance dashboard. It shows the information that matters to you — the current weather, the time and date, stock and cryptocurrency prices, your next calendar events, your latest emails, or a countdown to an important date — all in one place.


Everything is fully customizable: choose which widgets to show, pick one of seven color themes or create your own, and arrange the widgets however you like with Windows-style layouts. Make a widget bigger, move it around, or stack them however fits your screen.


What it does

  • Weather: live temperature and conditions for your location.
  • Date & Time: a large clock with the current date.
  • Stock / Crypto Prices: live prices and 24h changes.
  • Calendar: your upcoming Google Calendar events.
  • Notifications: your latest Gmail messages.
  • Countdown: counts down to a date you choose.

Private by design

Everything runs locally on your computer. Your settings are stored in a local file, and your data is only sent to the services that power the widgets (the weather service, price providers, and — only if you connect it — your own Google account). Nothing is collected or shared with third parties.


Getting started

  1. Start the local server (python server.py) and open this page.
  2. Press the "Settings" button in the menu and choose your widgets, theme, and layout.
  3. Press the "Save" button to save your changes.
  4. Press the "Open Dashboard" button in the menu, then press "Start Dashboard" to view it fullscreen.
  5. Enjoy your dashboard!
