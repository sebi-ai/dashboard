Dashboard — Your Personal Info Hub

Turns any screen into a live, at-a-glance dashboard: the current weather, the time, stock and crypto prices with charts, your calendar, your emails, and a personal countdown — arranged exactly how you like.

Pick your widgets, a theme, and a layout in Settings, press Save, then Open Dashboard to go fullscreen. Everything is customizable — up to six stocks and cryptos with 1D–3M charts included.

Your settings are stored only in your own browser — visitors can never change your dashboard, and your Google login never leaves your browser either:
the server only relays your own account's data to you.
